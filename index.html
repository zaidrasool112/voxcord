In plain terms

What is LinkedIn ghostwriting?

LinkedIn ghostwriting is a service where a writer turns a founder's expertise, experience, and opinions into LinkedIn posts published under the founder's own name. A LinkedIn ghostwriter interviews you, researches the topic, and drafts and edits each post in your voice, and you review and approve your posts. Ghostwriting on LinkedIn works like speechwriting: the ideas are yours, and someone else helps shape the words. At VOXCORD, every published sentence is written and edited by a person.

The actual gap

Right now, your expertise is real. Your LinkedIn presence may not show it.

Most of what makes you good at your work never makes it online. The lesson from a project that went sideways. The advice you give clients that actually changes how they think. The small things you've noticed that nobody else in your space has said out loud yet.

None of that shows up on your profile. And a quiet profile can read as inactive to someone deciding whether to reach out, however busy you actually are.

You don't need to become a content creator to fix this. You need someone to ask the right questions in a real conversation, then write it properly and keep it consistent, so you can get back to the actual work.

Here is how a LinkedIn ghostwriting engagement with VOXCORD works, from first review to monthly reporting.

We learn your business, your audience, and the reputation you're actually trying to build, and we agree what must stay confidential, before a single word gets written.

Human or machine

LinkedIn ghostwriter or AI writing tool?

AI tools generate drafts from a prompt or from your past posts. A human ghostwriter interviews you, so each post starts from something you actually said or did. VOXCORD may use AI tools for transcription, research, and early brainstorming, never to write the sentences that get published. If you already have plenty of posts and only need drafts, a tool may be enough. If you need your real experience drawn out, that takes a conversation.
