Published rates for LinkedIn ghostwriting run from about $500 to $10,000 or more a month (Windmill Growth). What drives the price

  • Post frequency: how many posts you get each month.
  • Research and interview depth: how much of your real experience is drawn out before writing starts.
  • What's included: whether profile work, engagement, and strategy are part of the package.
  • The writer's seniority and track record.

Choosing a provider

How to choose a LinkedIn ghostwriting service

Ask any provider who writes the posts, how they learn your voice, whether AI writes any of the published sentences, what they need from you, and who owns the content. Here is how we answer each.

WHO WRITES

A person writes every post: one of the two co-founders, with both of us going through the work. Meet the writers.

YOUR VOICE

We learn how you sound in a weekly conversation of 30 to 40 minutes, and every post comes with unlimited revisions on wording and tone until it sounds like you.

AI USE

AI may help with transcription, research, and early brainstorming. It never writes the sentences that get published. How we use AI.

WHAT WE NEED

About 30 to 40 minutes a week, or an agreed alternative, and your review and approval of your posts.

YOUR MATERIAL

Recordings and notes are used only to deliver your service, and anything you mark as confidential stays out of your posts. How we handle your material.

OWNERSHIP

You own the final, approved content once you've paid for it.
