1. The services

Agreement Section 1

VOXCORD provides LinkedIn ghostwriting and related services under the plan named in your signed agreement: Profile Foundation, Core, or Growth. Each plan's scope, deliverables, and current pricing are set out in the VOXCORD Service Overview that you receive before signing. The Service Overview is part of your agreement. You can read about the plans on our Services page.

Every published post is written by a person. We may use AI-assisted tools internally for research, transcription, and brainstorming, but AI never writes the sentences that get published in your posts. Where you authorize it, we also provide posting or scheduling support.

Not included: direct-message management, closing sales on your behalf, and any service outside your selected plan's scope.

2. Term and onboarding

Agreement Section 2

  • Core and Growth begin on the effective date and continue month to month until cancelled under section 5. Profile Foundation is a one-time engagement that ends on delivery, as described in the Service Overview.
  • Work begins within 24 hours of a signed agreement and a completed onboarding call or intake questionnaire.
  • You agree to be reasonably available for onboarding and for the weekly interviews, or an agreed alternative.

3. Fees and payment

Agreement Section 3

  • Core and Growth are billed monthly in advance, on the effective date and on the same date each month, unless we agree otherwise in writing.
  • Profile Foundation's full fee is due upfront, before work begins.
  • We accept the payment methods named in your agreement, or another method we agree in writing.
  • If a payment is late, work continues normally through day 7 after the due date. From day 8, we may pause active work until the account is current. After 30 days of non-payment, we may end the agreement by written notice.

4. Refunds

Agreement Section 4

  • A refund requested within 72 hours of the effective date receives a 90% refund of that billing cycle's fee. We keep the remaining 10% as a processing and cancellation fee, regardless of whether work has already begun or been delivered.
  • After the 72-hour window, no refund is available for that billing cycle, and service continues through the end of the paid period.

5. Cancellation and your data afterwards

Agreement Section 5

  • Either of us may cancel with written notice. Email is enough. Service continues through the end of the current paid billing cycle.
  • We keep interview transcripts, drafts, and working notes for 30 days after cancellation, in case you want to resume, and then delete them unless we agree otherwise. You can ask for earlier deletion at any time.

6. If you're unavailable

Agreement Section 6

If you miss interviews or go quiet for up to a week, we keep your profile active using your existing profile, the material you have already shared, and public information about your business. When you're back, we review everything published in that time and adjust or replace anything that misrepresents you, at no charge.

If we still haven't heard from you after a week, we pause posting until you return.

If we don't have authorization to post on your account, you remain responsible for publishing the content we deliver.

7. Confidentiality and what you provide

Agreement Section 7

Information you identify as confidential, such as client names, deal terms, internal strategy, or unreleased plans, is excluded from published content. If it isn't clear whether something is confidential, we confirm with you before publishing. We won't disclose your confidential information to any third party except as needed to deliver the services or as the law requires.

You are responsible for the accuracy, legality, and completeness of the information, stories, claims, and opinions you give us. You agree to indemnify VOXCORD against claims arising from inaccurate or misleading content you supply, which means covering us against those claims.

8. Who owns the content

Agreement Section 8

You own the final, approved content once the related invoice is paid. We keep no right to reuse, resell, or publish it elsewhere, except as you authorize under the case study and testimonial consent process described in the Service Overview.

Intermediate working materials, such as interview notes, research drafts, and internal planning documents, remain ours unless they are incorporated into final, paid content or we agree in writing to transfer them.

9. Account access and LinkedIn's rules

Agreement Section 9

We never request or use your LinkedIn password. We do not post, comment, or message on your behalf without your prior authorization. All our work stays within LinkedIn's Professional Community Policies, including its rules on authentic identity, account sharing, and artificial engagement.

We are not responsible for delays caused by events outside our reasonable control, including LinkedIn outages or policy changes. We will tell you about any material impact on the services.

10. No guarantee of results

Agreement Section 10

Our services are designed to improve your public communication, credibility, and consistency on LinkedIn. We do not guarantee reach, follower growth, lead volume, or business outcomes, which depend on factors outside our control. Performance reporting is provided as described in the Service Overview.

11. Limitation of liability

Agreement Section 11

Our total liability under the agreement is limited to the fees you paid in the three (3) months before a claim. We are not liable for indirect, incidental, or consequential damages, including lost business opportunities.

12. Data protection and privacy

Agreement Section 12

We collect and process your information, including interview recordings, notes, and business details, only to deliver the services in your agreement. We do not sell it or share it, except as needed to deliver the services or as the law requires. We record calls only with your signed consent.

You can ask for a copy of your data, or for its deletion, at any time, subject to the retention period in section 5.

13. Disputes and governing law

Agreement Sections 13 and 14

We will first try to resolve any dispute through good-faith negotiation. If it isn't resolved within 30 days, either of us may pursue resolution through the courts of the State of Delaware, United States.

The agreement is governed by the laws of the State of Delaware, United States, without regard to its conflict-of-laws principles.

14. General terms

Agreement Section 14

  • The signed agreement, together with the Service Overview and your selected plan, is the entire agreement between us and replaces any earlier discussions.
  • The agreement can only be changed by mutual written agreement, signed by both of us.
  • If any provision is found unenforceable, the remaining provisions stay in full effect.
